Questions

Frequently asked

Straight answers before you ever send a message.

How does pricing work for recurring lawn care?

We quote a flat per-visit rate based on lot size, slope, and how much trimming is involved. You get the number up front, and it only changes if the scope changes — which we confirm with you first.

Do you handle both residential and commercial properties?

Yes. Homes get a seasonal maintenance plan; commercial clients get contracts with documented visits, fixed monthly billing, and a dedicated account manager.

What happens in winter?

Maintenance pauses for the season and we offer snow plowing, clearing, salting, and ice management as an add-on for both homes and commercial lots.

Are you insured?

Fully licensed and insured, and we're happy to send a certificate of insurance to your property manager or HOA before work begins.

Can you take over messy beds and overgrown yards?

That's most of our first visits. We start with a one-time clean-up and reset, then move you onto a regular schedule that keeps it that way.
